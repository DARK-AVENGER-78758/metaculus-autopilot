## Practice betting with fake coins

Each betting rule started with 1,000 fake coins and trades by itself, 2% of its spare coins a bet, on markets closing within 14 days. It buys at the live asking price, sells at the live bid, and pays each market's real Polymarket fee both ways. It looks at every position again on each run. A share pays 1 coin if its side wins and nothing if it loses. No real money is involved. A rule earns a say after 40 closed bets if its gain per bet is clearly above zero (a margin of 2.5 or more); its new bets are then pointed out to you.

**Goal: turn 1,000 coins into 5,000 by winning bets, and in no other way.** The rules are fixed, a stake is always 2%, and a wallet that gets there stops opening new bets. "Follow the best" is a fifth wallet that copies whichever of the four rules has made the most per bet, once one has 10 closed bets and is ahead.
